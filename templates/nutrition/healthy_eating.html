Healthy eating

Meals, not math

No calorie counting required — just balanced, consistent choices.

PROTEIN

Building & repair

Chicken, fish, eggs, beans, tofu, dairy — aim for a portion at each meal.

CARBS

Steady energy

Whole grains, rice, potatoes, oats — fuel for workouts and daily activity.

FATS

Long-term health

Olive oil, nuts, avocado, oily fish — in moderate, consistent amounts.

PRODUCE

Fruit & vegetables

Aim to fill half your plate with a variety of colors.

FLUIDS

Hydration

Water throughout the day; more around exercise.

PORTIONS

Portion awareness

Eat until satisfied, not stuffed — no need to weigh every meal.
