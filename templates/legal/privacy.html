Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026. This explains what ThinkToFit collects and why.

What we collect

When you create an account and use ThinkToFit, we may collect: your name and email address; profile details you choose to provide (age, gender, height, weight, fitness goal, experience level); BMI calculations and history; your answers to the fitness-needs questionnaire; and any feedback or comments you submit.

Why we collect it

This information is used to: show you relevant exercise, weight-loss, and nutrition guidance; calculate and track your BMI; and, in aggregate and anonymized form, understand how well ThinkToFit meets users' needs as part of this project's research component.

Who can see it

Your personal profile and BMI history are visible only to you. The research/admin view shows aggregated statistics only — for example, the percentage of users with a given goal, or an average satisfaction rating — and does not link individual answers back to your identity.

How it's protected

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Data is only accessible to authenticated sessions, and administrative views require an admin account. We don't sell or share your data with third parties.

Your choices

You can update your profile information at any time from your dashboard. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact the site administrator.

This is a general fitness information platform built as an academic project. It is not a medical service, and this notice is provided for transparency about a student project rather than as a formal legal document.
