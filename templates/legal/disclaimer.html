Health disclaimer

ThinkToFit provides general fitness and healthy-lifestyle information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have a medical condition, injury, symptoms, or concerns about exercising or changing your diet, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Not a medical platform

ThinkToFit does not diagnose medical conditions, treat injuries, recommend medication, or replace the advice of a doctor, physiotherapist, or registered dietitian.

About the BMI calculator

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a general screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not account for muscle mass, body frame, or overall body composition, and shouldn't be used as the sole measure of your health. The calculator on this site is intended for adults aged 18 and over — it does not apply child or teen BMI categories, which require different, age- and sex-specific calculations.

About exercise and nutrition guidance

Exercise recommendations, weight-loss guidance, and meal plans on this site are general information intended for beginners and are not personalised clinical advice. They do not account for injuries, medical history, allergies, or other individual circumstances. Stop any exercise that causes pain and consult a professional before starting a new fitness or nutrition routine, especially if you are pregnant, have an existing health condition, or are returning from injury.

In an emergency

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately. Do not rely on this website for urgent medical guidance.
